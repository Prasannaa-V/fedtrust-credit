Why Explanation Drift Undermines Standard FedAvg

In standard federated averaging, models are weighted purely by sample count (Bank 1: 63%, Bank 2: 29%, Bank 3: 8%). If Bank 3 or Bank 2 overfits to its regional or subprime nuances, standard aggregation remains blind to its divergent decision boundaries. FedTrust-Credit computes TreeSHAP feature attributions on a reference anchor distribution, rewarding consensus agreement with peer explanations.
