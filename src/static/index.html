Why Explanation Drift Undermines Standard FedAvg

In standard federated averaging, models are weighted purely by sample count (Bank 1: 63%, Bank 2: 29%, Bank 3: 8%). If Bank 3 or Bank 2 overfits to its regional or subprime nuances, standard aggregation remains blind to its divergent decision boundaries. FedTrust-Credit computes TreeSHAP feature attributions on a reference anchor distribution, rewarding consensus agreement with peer explanations.

💡 Core Novelty (30-Second Elevator Pitch)

Standard FedAvg weights institutions purely by dataset size (ni). FedTrust-Credit collects TreeSHAP explanation consensus vectors across banks without sharing raw data. Clients aligning with consensus receive an aggregation gain factor (γ), boosting cross-institutional consistency (+68.4%) and naturally isolating Byzantine attackers without exposing customer records.

☁️ Multi-Instance Cloud Infrastructure

  • 2 Distributed EC2 Nodes: Primary Node (Aggregator + B1 + B2 on 13.218.90.189) & Remote Bank Node (Bank 3 Subprime) communicating over AWS IPv4 REST.
  • Amazon S3: fedtrust-models bucket for model serialization and registry.
  • Amazon CloudWatch: Namespace FedTrustCredit/FL logging real-time probability, consistency, and API latency.
  • Amazon SNS: Automated instant email alerts when borrower default probability ≥ 35%.
  • AWS IAM: Zero credentials in code; least-privilege EC2 instance profiles.

🎯 Top 3 Anticipated Viva Questions & Answers

Q1: Does transmitting SHAP vectors violate GDPR or leak private borrower data?
Answer: No. TreeSHAP vectors are 82 aggregated scalar feature-importance coefficients across an anchor distribution, not individual rows. Furthermore, we implemented calibrated Differential Privacy (ε-DP with Gaussian noise) to formally bound leakage.

Q2: How does the system defend against rogue or Byzantine banks?
Answer: If Bank 3 flips labels or poisons weights, its feature attribution flips, causing its Spearman agreement score to drop. The aggregator automatically slashes its aggregation weight from 25.0% down to ~2.1%, neutralizing the attack.

Q3: Why not pool the data into one central cloud database?
Answer: In real banking, GLBA, GDPR, and Fair Lending regulations strictly prohibit pooling raw borrower records across independent institutions. Federated learning enables collaborative training without data centralisation.
